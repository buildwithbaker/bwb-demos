Visit us

We're on Chequamegon Square in downtown Ashland, half a block up from the lake, in the tall brick building with the tin ceiling and the coffee smell. Here's how to find us, where to park, and when we're open.

Parking

Free two-hour street parking runs the length of Main Street, and there's a municipal lot behind the building off Second Avenue with no time limit. The loading zone out front is fair game for a quick run-in — flip on your flashers and grab your coffee. Bike rack's by the door.
