A proper general store on the shore of Lake Superior

Chequamegon Mercantile has stocked the useful, the local, and the hard-to-find on Chequamegon Square since the pantry ran on root cellars. Provisions, camp and cabin supplies, gifts worth giving, and shelves full of goods made by your neighbors.

What we carry

Four corners of the store, roughly. We restock what sells and chase down what you ask for — if we don't have it, there's a good chance we can get it.

Provisions & pantry

Coffee roasted up the road, Wisconsin cheese and sausage, maple syrup, jam, dry goods, and the bag of flour you forgot until you were already home.

Camp & cabin

Enamelware, wool socks, fire starters, bug dope, headlamps, and the odd hardware bit that keeps a north-woods cabin standing another season.

Local makers

Pottery, candles, soap, prints, and small-batch goods from artists and producers around Chequamegon Bay. New faces on the shelf most months.

Gifts & sundries

Cards, books about the lake, kids' odds and ends, and Lake Superior keepsakes that beat a gas-station magnet by a country mile.

Browse gifts & sundries

Made around the bay

More than sixty local makers share our shelves — the potter in Washburn, the soap-maker outside Bayfield, the fellow who ties flies through the long winter. When you buy their work here, the money stays in the county and the maker hears about it. That's the whole idea.
