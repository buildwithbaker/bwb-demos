Cabins & charters

Three log cabins strung along our stretch of Pike's Bay, each with a private dock and fire ring, plus two guided fishing trips out onto Lake Superior. Everything's cleaned by us, stocked by us, and answered by us.

The cabins

Tamarack Cabin

Sleeps 4 · 1 bedroom (queen) + queen sleeper sofa · 1 bath

Our cozy one-bedroom, tucked into the pines closest to the water. Full kitchen, woodstove, and a screened porch, with a private dock 40 steps from the door. Just right for a couple or a small family.

See ratesCheck dates

Birch Point Cabin

Sleeps 6 · 2 bedrooms (queen + two twins) + sleeper sofa · 1.5 baths

The middle cabin, set out on the point with water on two sides. Big open kitchen and living room, a wood-burning fireplace, and a wraparound deck built for morning coffee and evening card games.

See ratesCheck dates

Whitepine Lodge

Sleeps 8 · 3 bedrooms + loft · 2 baths

Our largest cabin, made for the whole gang or two families sharing. Vaulted great room, full-size kitchen, a long harvest table, and a fire pit down by the shore that seats a dozen.

Guided Lake Superior charters

Captain-run trips out of the Bayfield marina aboard a 28-foot Tiara rigged for big water. Charters aren't listed on Airbnb or VRBO — call or send an inquiry to get on the calendar.

Half-Day Lake Trout Charter

4 hours · morning or afternoon · up to 4 guests

The classic Apostle Islands trip. We troll the reefs and drop-offs for lake trout and the odd coho — a great first charter for kids and beginners. Rods, tackle, bait, and licenses are all handled; you bring a jacket and a cooler.

See ratesAsk about a trip

Full-Day Salmon & Trout Charter

8 hours · full day · up to 4 guests

A full day working the deep water for king and coho salmon plus lake trout, running out past the islands when the weather cooperates. Fish cleaning and bagging at the dock are included. This is the trip that fills the freezer.
