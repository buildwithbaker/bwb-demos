Rates & policies

Straight pricing, no resort fees. Cabin rates are per night; charter rates are per trip for up to four guests. Peak is June through August, shoulder covers May and September–October, and winter runs November through April.

What's included

  • Private dock and fire ring at your cabin, with a starter bundle of firewood
  • Full kitchen stocked with cookware, coffee maker, and basics — plus a gas grill
  • All linens and towels, made up before you arrive
  • Kayaks and a canoe to share, with life jackets in every size
  • On charters: rods, reels, tackle, bait, licenses, and fish cleaning at the dock
  • Wi-Fi at every cabin (spotty by design — you're up here to unplug)

Policies

Pets
Dogs are welcome at Tamarack Cabin (2 max, $25 per night). Birch Point and Whitepine are kept pet-free for guests with allergies.
Minimum stay
Two nights most of the year; three nights on summer weekends and holidays. Ask about last-minute one-night openings.
Cancellation
Full refund up to 14 days before arrival. Inside 14 days we refund whatever we're able to rebook. Charters run rain or shine, but we'll always reschedule for genuinely unsafe water.
