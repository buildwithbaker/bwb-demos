Privacy

Here's the plain-English version, because that's the only kind worth reading. Keweenaw Point Builders runs a small website. We're not in the data business, and we collect as little as we can.

The contact form

When you send us a message through the form on this site, it's delivered to our inbox by a service called Formspree, which simply passes it along. We use what you send only to get back to you about your project. We don't sell it, rent it, or hand it to anyone who isn't helping us answer you.

No ads, no tracking

We don't run ads. We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics that follow you around the web. There's no account to create and nothing to log into. Your browser keeps the ordinary records any website leaves behind, but we're not watching them.

Getting your information removed

If you've contacted us and you'd like us to delete what you sent, just ask. Email dale@keweenawpointbuilders.com and we'll remove it from our inbox and records. No forms, no hoops.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, email dale@keweenawpointbuilders.com or call the number in the footer. A real person will get back to you.
