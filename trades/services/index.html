Services

Everything below is work we do week in, week out. Timelines are honest averages, not best-case sales talk — weather and material lead times can stretch them, and we'll tell you up front if they will.

Kitchen remodels

Cabinets, counters, flooring, lighting — or the whole room down to the studs. We coordinate the plumber and electrician so you deal with one crew and one schedule.

Typical timeline: 3–5 weeks

Bathroom remodels

Tub-to-shower conversions, tile work, ventilation done right so you're not fighting mold in two years. We check for hidden rot before we quote, not after.

Typical timeline: 2–3 weeks

Decks & porches

Cedar, treated, or composite, built on footings below the frost line so it stays level. Railings and stairs to code, every time.

Typical timeline: 1–2 weeks

Windows & doors

Full-frame or insert replacements, flashed and insulated for a Lake Superior winter. You'll feel the difference the first cold snap.

Typical timeline: 2–4 days for most houses

Basement finishing

Family rooms, guest rooms, home offices. We deal with moisture first — insulation and vapor control before a single stud goes up.

Typical timeline: 4–6 weeks

Garages & pole buildings

Two-car garages, workshops, and storage buildings. Slab through shingles, sized for what you actually park and store.

Typical timeline: 3–6 weeks

Siding & exterior repair

Storm damage, soffit and fascia, rotten trim, full re-sides. We match existing materials where we can so patches don't look like patches.

Typical timeline: 1–2 weeks

Small jobs & fix-its

Sticky doors, deck boards, drywall patches, grab bars for the folks. No job too small — small jobs are how most of our customers found us.

Typical timeline: Half a day to two days
